- New (optional) online AI: a new "AI" page in settings, **off by default**; with it off the input method behaves exactly as before
- The selection translation (Ctrl+C+C) can run online: turn the switch on and tick "Selection translation" and the API does the work, falling back to the offline model on a timeout or a refusal ("online only" skips the fallback). About 30 services are built in (DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Kimi, GLM, Qwen, SiliconFlow, Groq, Ollama, OpenCode Go, ...), and your own address works too
- Keys are stored **DPAPI-encrypted**; several are kept per service and a refused one moves on to the next. Show/Hide reveals what is being typed, Detect recognises the service from the address, and Get models asks it for its list
- Thinking and the reasoning levels adapt to the service and model: each family's way of turning thinking off is used (DeepSeek and Zhipu take `thinking`, Qwen takes `enable_thinking`, ...), the level list follows the current service and model, and services without levels are greyed out. Turning thinking off is much faster (measured on one sentence: 4210 ms to about 1900 ms)
- The Test button shows how long the request took, in milliseconds, and the result
