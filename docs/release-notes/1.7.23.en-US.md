- The Learning page's candidate-translation card now has **one row per language** (Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Russian), each choosing what that language is translated into
- **The selection translation (Ctrl+C+C) follows those rows**: it finds the row of the language the selection is written in and translates into that row's language (it used to be hard-wired to Chinese into English, everything else into Chinese). Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Russian are told apart by their script; French, German and Spanish by a few characters (`ñ ¿ ß ä ç è œ` ...) and common words, falling back to English; a row that is off keeps the old rule, so **an unchanged configuration behaves exactly as before**
- The Chinese and English rows still show their translation after each candidate in the window (a row whose language pack is missing is gray); the other six rows only feed the selection translation and need no pack
- Narrower drop-downs, and the Learning page's hint no longer overlaps the text below it
