- The offline translation model is now **v3**: [LMT-60-0.6B](https://huggingface.co/NiuTrans/LMT-60-0.6B) with 8 of its 28 layers removed, distilled from Hy-MT2-7B in **nine languages** (Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Russian, Vietnamese). On three open test sets its COMET-22 is 8-11 points above v2 and answers not in the target language fall from about 6% to under 0.1%; the download is 322 MB (v2: 397 MB) and a page takes about 70 ms on the graphics card or 0.3 s on the CPU, both faster than v2. With v2 installed, click Check and then Update to v3 on the Learning page
- **Switching models no longer needs a restart**: before each page it has to translate, `zhiyu-server` checks which model is installed and, when another one was installed, stops the old model server, forgets its cached translations and starts the new one (before, the old model ran until it was unloaded when idle and its translations stayed cached until the IME server restarted). After an update, Settings ends the previous version's model server and deletes its folder at once (about 400 MB), instead of on the next update
