- **`\fy` now uses the ordinary candidate bar** instead of a panel of its own. Typing `\fy` puts the 12 languages in the candidate bar (nine to a page, `,` and `.` turn the pages), chosen exactly the way a word is: number keys, arrow keys with Space or Enter, Tab to cycle, or a mouse click. The choice is kept in `ai.translate_language` (the same setting as the drop-down on the AI page), **nothing is typed into the document**, and the composition ends
- The two protocol items that panel needed (`UiCommandType::kAiLanguageMenu`, `IPCCommand::SET_AI_LANGUAGE`), its code in the TSF and its vertical list are gone: the feature now has no window, no keys and no IPC command of its own
- "Summarize" still writes under the selected text (as of 1.7.33); the other actions are unchanged
