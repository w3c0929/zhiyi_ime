- **An action key now works with nothing selected**: it takes the text already on the clipboard, so a passage you just copied can be polished, proofread, rewritten, translated or summarized without selecting it first. The result goes where it always did — a replacing action at the caret, a summary as its own paragraph there
- **Only the last clipboard entry is taken**: the current clipboard text (the thing you copied last), never the clipboard's history and never several entries put together; anything that is not text (an image, files) counts as nothing
- With no text on the clipboard nothing happens at all: no window, nothing typed
- One stray key press is gone with it: with nothing selected we no longer send a `Ctrl+C` of our own (copying an empty selection makes some programs wipe the clipboard — exactly the text we were about to use). With a selection, nothing changes
- **Voice post-processing works now**: tick "Voice post-processing" on the AI page and the recognized words go through the AI "Proofread" action first — **the corrected text is what gets typed**. The voice key, the recording, the overlay and the key handling are untouched; "recognizing" simply stays up a moment longer (the length of that one request)
- Anything in the way falls back to the recognized words as they are: the master switch off, this purpose unticked, no key, a failed or timed-out request, or more than the action's 8000-character limit — nothing you said is ever lost
- **The "English completion and rewriting" purpose is gone** (it was never implemented): the AI page now has four purpose checkboxes — selection translation, AI actions on the selection, voice post-processing and the language-pack fallback. An `ai.use_for.english` left in an older configuration is ignored
