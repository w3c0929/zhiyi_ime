- **What a key works on is now looked for in three places, in this order**: (1) the selection; (2) with nothing selected, **the caret's line from its start to the caret** (selected for you and put on the clipboard); (3) when neither has anything, **the clipboard** — what you copied with `Ctrl+C` at some point
- So "copy something, go to an empty box (or a program that will not give its text up), press the key" works on what you copied, without pasting it first
- **The clipboard comes last**: it never overrides the selection or the line at your caret, so the key cannot end up handling something else while you were pointing at your own text; and when it does use the clipboard, the text being handled is right there in the window (the line under "正在润色…")
- With nothing to work on, the message says so plainly: 没有可处理的内容（没选中、光标这一行没字、剪贴板里也没有文本）
- **A password field is never touched**: not read, and the clipboard is not used in its place
