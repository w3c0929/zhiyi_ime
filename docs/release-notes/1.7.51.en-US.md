- **Fix (the real one): in Sublime Text and programs like it, our copy always arrived one press late.** Measured: the injected `Ctrl+C` **does work** (sent from another process, the clipboard's sequence moved 232→238), but our read runs **on the program's own thread** (TSF key handling is called there), so those three keys merely sit in its **message queue** until this handler returns — and the handler was waiting for the clipboard, so it never saw the copy. That explains **every** symptom so far: why your own Ctrl+C worked (the thread was idle and acted at once), and why it was hit and miss (the press before's copy was read by the press after — one press late)
- **The change**: the wait now **runs the program's messages** (`wait_for_program_copy`) instead of sleeping through them; keys that arrive while waiting belong to the program (`_readingSource`), not to the input method's key handling
- So in such a program: **its copy is asked for, waited for, and what it actually copied is what gets worked on** — no longer whatever the clipboard happened to hold
