- **The word choice now scores a word n-gram (ng1017)**: every whole-input candidate gains "how likely it is after the text before it" from a 79 MB word trigram model (shipped with the installer, mapped into memory rather than read onto the heap). End to end after the pause: long input **67.2% → 74.5%**, Chinese words **86.2% → 87.6%**; the cost is about +9 ms on a page with no context and +10 ms on a long one (CPU)
- **It has a tick of its own** (Settings > General, under "Recommend candidates and compose long input by context"): **Word n-gram**, on by default (`laya.ngram`). Untick it and the 79 MB file is not loaded at all — a page with nothing before the caret no longer pays for it (about 9 ms a page) — at the cost of the long-input gain. It is greyed out while the recommendation itself is off
- **Sentences the composition head builds now go through the word choice too** (`laya.continuation_rerank`, on by default; upstream measured long input typed in full 61.8% → 68.7%)
- **The recommendation model is now Zhiyi 1.4V2-Long-L12** (the same file as upstream 1.5.12's default; re-published here as `model-zhen-long-l12`): 1.4V2 keeps 12 of its 22 encoder layers, its word choice was distilled on 3.77M rows of the IME's real candidates (1.7M before). **With the word n-gram the accuracy ties Long-L18**, while being **faster and smaller**: about 14.7 → 10.4 ms per page on the CPU, and a 96 → 84 MB model file. The vocabulary, tokenizer and sequence format are unchanged, so the engine needed no change
