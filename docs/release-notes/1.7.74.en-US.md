- **Voice input now offers three recognition models** (Settings > Voice, "Recognition model"):
  - **SenseVoice Small (INT8, default)**: the fastest, about 1 GB of memory, still runs on a graphics card;
  - **Fun-ASR-Nano**: steadier on dialect, accent and speech away from the microphone; about 973 MB, CPU only, roughly 5x slower than SenseVoice and about 3 GB of memory;
  - **Qwen3-ASR 0.6B**: better on technical terms in Chinese mixed with English; about 952 MB, CPU only, same speed and memory as the above.
- The two larger models are **downloaded when first selected** (from this repository's `voice` release, every file checked against its SHA-256) and can be removed separately; installing and switching happen in Settings and take effect on the next dictation.
- The installer now ships the **sherpa-onnx runtime** they share (`voice-runtime\`, with an ONNX Runtime of its own so it never meets the recommendation model's DirectML one).
- SenseVoice, its graphics-card test and the voice-key flow are unchanged.
